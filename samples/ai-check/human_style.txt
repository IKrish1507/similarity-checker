I got my first hive two summers ago, mostly by accident. A neighbour was moving to Perth and didn't want to take a box of angry bees across the Nullarbor, so she asked if I'd look after it. I said yes before I'd really thought about it.

The first month was rough. I got stung four times, once on the eyelid, which was not a good look at work. I also had no idea what I was doing during inspections, so I'd pull frames out, stare at them, and put them back. Were those queen cells? Was that much drone brood normal? I honestly couldn't tell you.

Things got better once I joined the local club. The old guys there have been keeping bees for forty years and they'll happily talk for an hour about mites. One of them came round, took one look at my hive and said it was about to swarm. He was right. Two days later half my bees were hanging off the neighbour's lemon tree.

Now I've got three hives on the back fence. The honey changes every few months, darker in winter and almost clear in spring when the jacaranda's out. My partner still won't go near them. Fair enough, really.
