Cities are changing how we think about wildlife. Urban beekeeping has grown quickly over the last decade, as more people look for ways to support pollinators in cities! Beekeepers in dense suburbs often notice that their honey tastes different from season to season because the flowers in bloom change throughout the year.

Many new beekeepers start with a single hive in the backyard and learn by watching more experienced members of their local club. Hives need regular inspections to check for pests, disease and signs that the colony is preparing to swarm. Some people prefer native stingless bees, which are smaller, calmer and better suited to warm coastal climates.
